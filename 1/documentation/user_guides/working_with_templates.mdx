# Working with Templates

## Introduction

This guide provides an overview of how to create and use templates effectively, focusing on the updated manuscript templates provided by the Telematica journal. These templates are designed to align with Open Science policies and facilitate the submission process for authors.

## Telematica Journal Templates

### New Template Announcement

The Telematica journal has introduced an updated template for manuscript submissions. This update is part of the journal's commitment to maintaining high standards in scientific publishing and aligning with Open Science policies. Authors are encouraged to utilize these templates to ensure their submissions meet the journal's requirements.

- **Announcement Date:** July 30, 2022
- **Access the Templates:** [For Authors](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/information/authors)

### Guidelines for Authors

To ensure a smooth submission process, authors should adhere to the following guidelines when using the Telematica journal templates:

1. **Content Relevance:** Submissions should be current and relevant to the field of Telecommunications/ICTs.
2. **Author Identification:** Include the names of all authors along with their ORCID identifiers and institutional affiliations.
3. **Manuscript Structure:** Follow the instructions provided in the journal's template for structuring and submitting manuscripts.
4. **Author Contributions:** Clearly report the contributions of each author using the CRediT taxonomy.

For more detailed guidelines, authors can refer to the journal's submission requirements.

- **Announcement Date:** June 13, 2021
- **Read More:** [Quarterly Publication Announcement](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/announcement/view/15)

## Summary

This guide has outlined the key aspects of working with the updated templates provided by the Telematica journal. By following the guidelines and utilizing the templates, authors can ensure their submissions are well-structured and compliant with the journal's standards.
