# Compliance Standards

## Introduction

This documentation provides an overview of the compliance standards and ethical guidelines related to scientific and technical publications, specifically within the context of the Telemática journal. The journal emphasizes transparency, ethical responsibilities, and adherence to recognized standards to ensure the quality and integrity of scientific communication.

## Authorship and Reviewer Guidelines

### Authorship Roles and Responsibilities

The Telemática journal follows the CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) system, which identifies 14 different roles of authorship or contribution. These roles include project management, formal analysis, conceptualization, data curation, writing (first draft and review/editing), research, methodology, obtaining funding, resources, software, monitoring, validation, and visualization. All authors are equally responsible for the article, and any conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

### Reviewer Responsibilities

Reviewers are expected to evaluate manuscripts objectively, respecting the intellectual independence of the authors. They should provide clear, truthful, and convincing feedback, especially when offering negative judgments. Reviewers must disclose any conflicts of interest and report any instances of plagiarism or data falsification to the journal's management.

## Ethical Guidelines and Submission Requirements

### Transparency and Ethical Practices

The journal adheres to the principles of transparency and good practices in scholarly publishing as recommended by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and other organizations. Authors are required to accurately identify themselves with ORCID identifiers and disclose their institutional affiliations.

### Manuscript Structure and Contribution

Manuscripts must be structured according to the journal's template, and authors must clearly report their specific contributions to the research and manuscript preparation. The CRediT taxonomy is used to specify these contributions.

## Certifications and Recognitions

### ORCID Requirement

Authors must maintain an updated ORCID profile to publish in the journal. This requirement ensures accurate author identification and supports the integrity of the publication process.

### Latindex and CITMA Certifications

The Telemática journal has been certified by Latindex and the Ministry of Science, Technology, and Environment (CITMA) for meeting high standards in scientific and technological publication. These certifications recognize the journal's contribution to research and innovation in telecommunications and electronics.

## Summary

This documentation outlines the compliance standards and ethical guidelines followed by the Telemática journal. By adhering to recognized standards and maintaining transparency and ethical practices, the journal ensures the integrity and quality of its scientific communications. The certifications from Latindex and CITMA further underscore the journal's commitment to excellence in research and publication.

For more detailed information, please refer to the [Telemática journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
