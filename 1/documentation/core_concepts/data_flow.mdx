# Data Flow Overview

This documentation provides an overview of how data moves through the system, focusing on the core concepts and architecture involved in data flow. It is crucial for understanding the fundamental processes that underpin the system's operation.

## Introduction

Data flow within a system is a critical aspect of its architecture, influencing efficiency, performance, and scalability. This overview will explore the methodologies and technologies involved in managing and optimizing data flow, particularly in the context of energy efficiency and network management.

## Energy Efficiency in Data Centers

### Integrated Architecture for Energy Metrics

Data centers are significant consumers of energy, necessitating precise evaluation metrics to ensure efficiency. Traditional metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) have limitations, prompting the development of more integrated approaches.

- **Layered Integration Architecture**: This architecture is proposed to address the heterogeneity of energy data and support accurate, near real-time measurement of energy efficiency metrics. It allows for a comprehensive assessment that links energy consumption to service quality.

- **Evaluation Tools**: Various commercial and open-source tools are compared based on accuracy, usability, and cost-effectiveness to estimate PUE and other metrics.

For more detailed information, refer to the article on energy metrics in data centers: [Integrated Architecture Proposal](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1025).

## Network Management

### Information-Centric Networks vs. IP Networks

The evolution of network architectures is crucial for meeting the demands of modern applications such as IoT and vehicular networks. Information-Centric Networks (ICN) offer several advantages over traditional IP networks:

- **Efficiency and Security**: ICN provides native content management, greater distribution efficiency, and enhanced data-level security.
- **Scalability and Flexibility**: These networks are more adaptable to dynamic environments, supporting mobility and scalability.

The transition to ICN presents challenges and opportunities, particularly in integrating with existing IP networks. For a comprehensive analysis, see the comparative study of network architectures: [Comparative Study of Networks](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1022).

## IoT Systems

### Monitoring and Data Collection

IoT systems play a pivotal role in data collection and monitoring, exemplified by their application in the tobacco industry:

- **IoT Platform Design**: Utilizing Raspberry Pi and SHT85 thermo-hygrometer probes, the system enables remote monitoring of the tobacco curing process. This setup reduces costs and reliance on imported equipment.

- **Data Registration**: The system records data in an IoT cloud platform, accessible from anywhere, enhancing the efficiency of monitoring processes.

For further insights into IoT applications in industry, refer to the article on IoT systems for monitoring: [IoT System for Tobacco Monitoring](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/599).
