# Key Concepts

## Introduction

This documentation provides an overview of essential terminology and concepts related to the Telemática journal, focusing on its open-access policies, ethical guidelines, and the scope of its publications. Telemática is a scientific-technical journal dedicated to advancing knowledge in the field of Telecommunications and Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs).

## About Telemática

Telemática is an open-access journal published by the Havana University of Technology "José Antonio Echeverría" (CUJAE). It aims to disseminate research findings and enhance theoretical and practical knowledge in telecommunications. The journal is continuously updated and indexed in several major databases, including [Latindex 2.0](https://www.latindex.org/latindex/ficha?folio=23159), [Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)](https://doaj.org/toc/1729-3804?source=%7B%22query%22%3A%7B%22bool%22%3A%7B%22must%22%3A%5B%7B%22terms%22%3A%7B%22index.issn.exact%22%3A%5B%221729-3804%22%5D%7D%7D%5D%7D%7D%2C%22size%22%3A100%2C%22sort%22%3A%5B%7B%22created_date%22%3A%7B%22order%22%3A%22desc%22%7D%7D%5D%2C%22_source%22%3A%7B%7D%2C%22track_total_hits%22%3Atrue%7D), [Periodica](https://periodica.dgb.unam.mx/F/7ECBLG73GBLL86KTEIRUDCT6SPU9KGFBRHACPBAAD79NCRIFUJ-09194?func=find-acc&acc_sequence=001278824), and [CITMA](https://www.citma.gob.cu).

## Open Access and Licensing

Telemática adheres to open access principles, providing free and immediate access to its content. The journal supports the exchange of global knowledge by allowing the use and adaptation of its articles under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 Unported License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

## Ethical Guidelines and Authorship

The journal emphasizes transparency and ethical practices in scholarly publishing. Authors must accurately report their contributions using the CRediT taxonomy, which includes roles such as project management, formal analysis, and data curation. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed, and manuscripts should adhere to IEEE referencing standards.

## Open Science Practices

Telemática promotes open science to accelerate research communication. Authors can use preprint servers for their articles, and the journal encourages data sharing and transparent peer review processes. Reviewers are expected to evaluate manuscripts objectively and disclose any conflicts of interest.

## Focus and Scope

The journal covers a wide range of topics within Telecommunications and ICTs, including:

- Communications and wireless systems
- Network management and services
- Internet technologies and services
- Security in telecommunications
- Digital signal processing and pattern recognition

## Summary

Telemática is a key resource for academics, researchers, and students in the field of Telecommunications and ICTs. Its commitment to open access, ethical publishing, and open science practices ensures that it remains a valuable platform for scientific communication and development.

For more information, visit the [Telemática journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
