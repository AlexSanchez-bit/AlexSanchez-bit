# System Limitations

## Introduction

This documentation page outlines known limitations and constraints within various systems and technologies, as discussed in recent scholarly articles. The focus is on understanding the challenges faced by traditional and emerging systems, particularly in network management, data center energy efficiency, and authentication methods.

## Network Management Limitations

Traditional IP network architectures, which have been foundational to the Internet's development, are increasingly facing limitations due to the demands of modern applications such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and vehicular networks. These limitations include:

- **Addressing and Routing**: Traditional models struggle with the dynamic and scalable requirements of modern applications.
- **Traffic Control and Monitoring**: Existing systems may not efficiently handle the high volume and variety of data traffic.
- **Security and Scalability**: There are inherent challenges in securing data and scaling networks to meet growing demands.

Emerging Information-Centric Networks (ICN) offer potential solutions by enabling native content management and improved data-level security, but transitioning to these models presents its own set of challenges.

For more detailed insights, refer to the article on [Comparative Study of Information-Centric Networks and IP Networks](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1022).

## Data Center Energy Efficiency

The energy consumption of data centers is a critical concern, with traditional metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) showing limitations. Key issues include:

- **Accuracy and Usability**: Existing tools for estimating PUE may lack precision and user-friendliness.
- **Real-Time Data Collection**: There is a need for architectures that support accurate, near real-time energy metric collection and analysis.

A proposed layered integration architecture aims to address these limitations by providing a more comprehensive assessment of energy efficiency in data centers.

For further reading, see the article on [Integrated Architecture Proposal for Energy Metrics in Data Centers](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1025).

## Authentication Systems

Traditional alphanumeric passwords are increasingly seen as inadequate due to their security vulnerabilities. Alternatives like Passpoints-based graphical authentication systems have been developed to address these issues. However, these systems also face limitations:

- **Security and Functionality**: While offering improved security, graphical authentication systems must be adaptable across different devices and screen resolutions.
- **Implementation Challenges**: The development of effective discretization methods is crucial for translating these systems into practical applications.

For more information, refer to the article on [Development of a Passpoints-Based Graphical Authentication API](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/970).

## Summary

This documentation highlights the limitations and challenges faced by traditional systems in network management, data center energy efficiency, and authentication methods. Emerging technologies and methodologies offer potential solutions, but they also bring new challenges that must be addressed for effective implementation.

For more detailed studies and discussions, please refer to the respective articles linked above.
