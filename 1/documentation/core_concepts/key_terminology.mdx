# Key Terminology

## Introduction

This documentation page provides important terms and definitions related to the journal Telemática, an open-access scientific-technical publication dedicated to the field of Telecommunications and Information and Communication Technologies (ICT). The journal aims to advance theoretical and practical knowledge, fostering technological and scientific development both nationally and internationally.

## Important Terms and Definitions

### Open Access Policy

- **Open Access**: Telemática provides immediate free access to its content under the principle that making research freely available supports a greater exchange of global knowledge. The journal uses Creative Commons licenses to formalize open access:
  - **CC-BY International**: Allows the exchange, use, and adaptation of articles with proper credit to the authors.
  - **CC0**: Applies to the metadata of articles, making it available upon request.

### Authorship and Contribution

- **CRediT Taxonomy**: A specification system for detailing author contributions in research. It includes 14 roles such as project management, formal analysis, conceptualization, data curation, writing, research, methodology, and more. This taxonomy is maintained by the Consortia for the Advancement of Standards in Research Administration Information (CASRAI).

### Peer Review Process

- **Evaluation Phases**: Manuscripts undergo a three-phase evaluation process:
  - **Editorial Evaluation**: Conducted by the Executive Editor to ensure compliance with submission rules.
  - **Academic Evaluation**: Assesses the scientific and intellectual quality of the manuscript.
  - **Final Evaluation**: Concludes the review process before publication.

### Ethical Guidelines

- **Transparency and Ethics**: Authors must disclose conflicts of interest and adhere to ethical standards in research and publication. The journal emphasizes transparency and integrity in scientific communication.

### Open Science Practices

- **Preprints and Data Sharing**: Authors can use preprint servers for articles ready for submission, allowing for data sharing and transparency in research processes. This practice supports methodological rigor and cooperation among researchers.

## Summary

This documentation page outlines key terminologies associated with the journal Telemática, focusing on its open access policy, authorship guidelines, peer review process, and ethical standards. The journal is committed to promoting open science practices and ensuring transparency and integrity in scientific communication.

For more detailed information, please visit the [Telemática Journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
