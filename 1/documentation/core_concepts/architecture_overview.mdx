# Architecture Overview

## Introduction

This documentation provides an overview of system architectures, focusing on the comparative analysis of traditional IP network management and emerging information-centric network (ICN) architectures, as well as an integrated architecture proposal for energy metric collection in data centers. These architectures address modern challenges in network efficiency, scalability, and energy management.

## Information-Centric Networks vs. IP Networks

### Overview

Traditional IP network architecture has been the backbone of Internet development for decades. However, it faces limitations with the increasing demands of modern applications such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and vehicular networks. Information-Centric Networks (ICN) are emerging as a more efficient and flexible alternative.

### Key Advantages of ICN

- **Efficiency**: ICN enables native content management, improving distribution efficiency.
- **Security**: Offers data-level security, enhancing protection against various threats.
- **Scalability**: Better suited for dynamic environments, supporting mobility and large-scale deployments.
- **Integration**: Opportunities for hybrid IP-ICN integration using technologies like Software-Defined Networking (SDN).

### Challenges and Opportunities

The transition to ICN presents several challenges, including the need for new addressing, routing, and traffic control strategies. However, it also offers opportunities for improved network management and deployment strategies.

For further reading, refer to the [Comparative Study of Information-Centric Networks and IP Networks](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1022).

## Integrated Architecture for Data Center Energy Metrics

### Introduction

Data centers are experiencing rising energy consumption, necessitating precise evaluation of energy efficiency. Traditional metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) have limitations that need addressing.

### Proposed Architecture

A layered integration architecture is proposed to enhance the accuracy and real-time collection of energy metrics. This architecture supports:

- **Heterogeneity Management**: Addresses the diverse nature of energy data.
- **Real-Time Analysis**: Enables near real-time presentation of energy efficiency measurements.

### Evaluation Tools

The architecture compares commercial and open-source tools for estimating PUE based on accuracy, usability, and cost-effectiveness, providing a comprehensive assessment of energy management strategies.

For more details, refer to the [Integrated Architecture Proposal for the Collection and Processing of Energy Metrics in Data Centers](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1025).

## Summary

This documentation highlights the evolving landscape of network and data center architectures. Information-Centric Networks offer a promising alternative to traditional IP networks, addressing modern demands for efficiency, security, and scalability. Meanwhile, the proposed integrated architecture for data centers aims to improve energy efficiency through accurate and real-time metric collection and analysis. These advancements reflect ongoing efforts to optimize network and data center operations in response to contemporary challenges.
