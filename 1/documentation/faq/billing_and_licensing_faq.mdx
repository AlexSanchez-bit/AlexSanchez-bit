# Billing and Licensing FAQ

## Introduction

This document provides answers to frequently asked questions regarding the billing and licensing policies of the Telemática journal. As an open-access journal, Telemática offers unique policies that promote the free exchange of knowledge and adhere to open science practices.

## Open Access Policy

Telemática is committed to providing immediate free access to its content. This open-access model supports the principle that making research freely available to the public encourages a greater exchange of global knowledge. The journal does not charge authors for the submission, processing, or publication of articles.

### Licensing

The content of the Telemática journal is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 Unported License. This license allows others to share and adapt the work non-commercially, as long as they attribute the authorship of the work and the initial publication in the journal. More details about the license can be found [here](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

## Author Rights and Responsibilities

Authors who publish in Telemática retain the copyright of their work. They grant the journal the right to be the first publisher of their work under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivativeWorks 3.0 Unported License. This license permits others to share the work with proper acknowledgment but does not allow for derivative works.

Authors are encouraged to share their work in institutional repositories or on personal websites to increase visibility and citation. They may also enter into additional agreements for non-exclusive distribution of the published version of their work, provided that the initial publication in Telemática is acknowledged.

## Open Science Practices

Telemática aligns itself with open science practices to accelerate research communication and enhance transparency. Authors are encouraged to:

1. Use the preprint modality for articles, allowing them to be deposited in preprint servers before or during submission to the journal.
2. Properly cite and reference the underlying contents of their articles to facilitate data and method sharing.
3. Participate in a progressive peer review process that offers the option for open dialogue with referees.

## Peer Review Process

Manuscripts submitted to Telemática undergo a rigorous peer review process, which includes:

- **Editorial Evaluation:** Initial verification by the Executive Editor to ensure compliance with submission guidelines.
- **Academic Evaluation:** Detailed assessment by academic peers.
- **Final Evaluation:** Concluding review to finalize the manuscript's suitability for publication.

## Summary

Telemática is a bilingual, open-access journal that supports free publication and open science practices. It does not charge authors for article processing and encourages the sharing and adaptation of its content under specific Creative Commons licenses. Authors retain copyright and are encouraged to disseminate their work widely, fostering greater knowledge exchange and collaboration.

For more detailed information, please visit the [Telemática journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
