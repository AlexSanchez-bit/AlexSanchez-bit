## Donation Page Features

The donation page on the Red Sobrevivientes website offers several key features to facilitate contributions:

- **Donation Options and Amounts**: Users can choose from various donation amounts.
- **Payment Provider Integration**: The site supports payments through PayPal, debit, and credit cards.
- **Interactive Elements**: Includes navigation links, donation amount buttons, and payment method buttons.

For more details, visit the [donation page](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app/#/donate).

## Testimonial Sharing Modal

The website provides a platform for survivors to share their stories through a testimonial-sharing modal. This feature is accessible by clicking the "Sí, soy Sobreviviente" button on the homepage. Key features include:

- **Initial Options**: Users can select "Soy Sobreviviente" or "No soy sobreviviente, pero conozco uno."
- **Form Fields**: Similar to the "Comparte tu historia" page, with options to cancel or submit the testimony.
- **Action Buttons**: Includes 'Cancelar', 'Enviar testimonio', and a close button.

## Navigation and Access Limitations

While navigating the Red Sobrevivientes website, users may encounter certain limitations:

- **Blog Page**: Displays "No hay nada que mostrar" indicating no content available.
- **Navigation Links**: Links such as "Nuestros Aliados" and "Comparte tu historia" do not update the URL hash, remaining on the base URL (/#/).

For more information on navigation, visit the [homepage](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app/#/).
