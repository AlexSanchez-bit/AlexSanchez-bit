# Technical FAQ

This document provides detailed answers to frequently asked technical questions related to the Telemática journal, a scientific-technical publication focused on Telecommunications and Information and Communication Technologies (ICT). The journal is open access and continuously updated, aiming to advance theoretical and practical knowledge in its field.

## About Telemática

Telemática is a scientific-technical journal published by the Havana University of Technology "José Antonio Echeverría" (CUJAE). It focuses on the broad field of Telematics and Telecommunications, featuring state-of-the-art research and advancements. The journal is aimed at academics, researchers, and students, and strives to disseminate research to increase theoretical and practical knowledge in telecommunication systems.

- **Open Access**: Telemática provides immediate free access to its content, supporting a greater exchange of global knowledge. It uses Creative Commons licenses to formalize this access.
- **Publication Frequency**: Articles are published continuously as soon as they are approved and edited.

## Editorial and Review Process

### Submission and Evaluation

1. **Editorial Evaluation**: Conducted by the Executive Editor to ensure compliance with journal guidelines. This phase lasts up to 15 days and results in:
   - Rejection if guidelines are not met.
   - Request for modifications.
   - Acceptance for academic peer review.

2. **Academic Evaluation**: Involves peer review by at least two reviewers, with a possible third in case of conflicting opinions. The review process can involve up to three rounds over four months.

3. **Final Evaluation**: After editing, the article is sent to the author for final approval, with only minor changes allowed.

### Modifications and Decisions

- **Minimal Modifications**: Include ordering bibliographic references and ensuring consistent wording.
- **Moderate Modifications**: May involve clarifying materials and methods or reorganizing the article structure.
- **Complex Modifications**: Require significant changes such as updating the bibliography or improving the discussion of results.

## Open Science and Peer Review

Telemática aligns with open science practices to enhance research communication and transparency. Authors can use preprint servers, and the journal encourages data sharing and transparent peer review processes.

## Journal History and Sponsorship

- **History**: Originating from a Networking Bulletin (1999-2002), Telemática was established in 2002 to provide Cuban professionals with scientific and technical information. It adopted the Open Journal System in 2012 to enhance knowledge dissemination.
- **Sponsors**: Supported by organizations such as the [Ministerio de las Comunicaciones](http://www.mincom.gob.cu/), [Tecnomática](https://tecnomatica.cupet.cu/), and [ETECSA](http://www.etecsa.cu/).

## Summary

Telemática is a key resource for those in the field of Telecommunications and ICT, offering open access to cutting-edge research. Its structured editorial and peer review processes ensure the publication of high-quality articles. The journal's commitment to open science practices further supports the global exchange of knowledge.

For more information, visit the [Telemática journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
