# General Best Practices

## Introduction

This guide provides an overview of general best practices in scholarly publishing, focusing on ethical guidelines, transparency, and the promotion of open science. These practices are essential for maintaining the integrity and quality of scientific communication.

## Ethical Guidelines and Transparency

### Author Guidelines

1. **Manuscript Requirements**: Authors should ensure that their papers present current and relevant content in the field of Telecommunications/ICTs. The work should be concise, accurate, and objectively discuss its scientific significance.

2. **Author Identification**: All authors must register their names with unique ORCID identifiers and provide their institutional affiliations, including geographic location and email addresses.

3. **Manuscript Structure**: Follow the journal's template for structuring manuscripts and supplementary materials. This ensures consistency and clarity in submissions.

4. **Author Contributions**: Clearly report the contributions of each author using the CRediT taxonomy, which includes roles such as project management, formal analysis, and writing.

5. **Conflict of Interest**: Authors must disclose any potential conflicts of interest related to the research.

6. **Referencing**: Use the IEEE standard for bibliographic references to ensure consistency and accuracy.

7. **Preprints**: Manuscripts previously deposited in accepted preprint servers are allowed, provided the server name and URL are disclosed.

8. **Open Science Compliance**: Submit the Open Science Compliance Form as a supplementary file to promote transparency and data sharing.

### Reviewer Guidelines

1. **Objective Evaluation**: Reviewers should assess manuscripts based on their merits and provide constructive feedback without personal bias.

2. **Confidentiality**: The content of the manuscript is the property of the authors and should not be used or disclosed without consent.

3. **Conflict of Interest**: Reviewers must disclose any conflicts of interest and avoid reviewing manuscripts where such conflicts exist.

4. **Plagiarism Detection**: If plagiarism or data falsification is suspected, reviewers should inform the journal management confidentially.

## Open Access and Open Science

### Open Access Policy

Telemática is an open access journal, providing free access to its content to support global knowledge exchange. Articles are published under Creative Commons licenses, allowing use and adaptation with proper attribution.

### Open Science Practices

1. **Preprint Modality**: Authors can use preprint servers to share their articles before or during submission to the journal.

2. **Data Sharing**: Authors should properly cite and reference all underlying content to facilitate data exchange and collaboration.

3. **Peer Review Process**: The journal offers a progressive peer review process, allowing for direct dialogue between authors and reviewers and the option to publish review opinions.

## Summary

This guide highlights the importance of ethical practices, transparency, and open science in scholarly publishing. By adhering to these guidelines, authors and reviewers contribute to the integrity and advancement of scientific research.

For more detailed information, please refer to the [Telemática journal guidelines](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
