# Security Best Practices

## Introduction

This guide provides security recommendations and guidelines, focusing on best practices to ensure the integrity and protection of information within various technological environments. The content is synthesized from the Telemática journal's documentation, which emphasizes ethical standards, transparency, and security in telecommunications and information technology.

## Ethical Guidelines and Transparency

The Telemática journal adheres to rigorous ethical standards to maintain transparency and integrity in scientific publishing. These guidelines are crucial for authors, reviewers, and editors to ensure the quality and reliability of published research.

- **Author Identification and Contributions**: Authors must register their names with unique ORCID identifiers and provide institutional affiliations. Contributions should be detailed using the CRediT taxonomy, which includes roles such as project management, formal analysis, and data curation.

- **Conflict of Interest Disclosure**: It is essential to declare any potential conflicts of interest in the research and publication process to maintain transparency and trust.

- **Plagiarism Detection**: The journal employs anti-plagiarism measures to ensure originality. Any suspicion of plagiarism or scientific fraud results in the withdrawal of the manuscript from the editorial process.

## Security in Telecommunications and ICT

Security is a critical aspect of telecommunications and information and communication technologies (ICT). The Telemática journal addresses various security concerns and provides insights into emerging technologies and practices.

- **Information-Centric Networks (ICN)**: Compared to traditional IP networks, ICNs offer enhanced security, scalability, and efficiency, particularly in dynamic environments like IoT and vehicular networks. The transition to content-centric networking presents opportunities for improved data-level security and mobility support.

- **Graphical Authentication**: The development of Passpoints-based graphical authentication systems highlights the need for secure and user-friendly authentication methods. These systems address the limitations of traditional alphanumeric passwords and are adaptable across various devices and screen resolutions.

## Open Access and Data Sharing

The Telemática journal promotes open access to research and encourages the sharing of data to enhance scientific communication and collaboration.

- **Open Research Data**: The journal supports the initiative of making research data available in public repositories, facilitating transparency and reproducibility in scientific research.

- **Preprint Servers**: Manuscripts deposited in preprint servers are accepted, provided they are disclosed during submission. This practice supports the rapid dissemination of research findings.

## Summary

This guide outlines the security best practices derived from the Telemática journal's standards and guidelines. By adhering to ethical principles, ensuring transparency, and embracing open access, researchers and practitioners can enhance the security and integrity of their work in telecommunications and ICT.

For more detailed information, refer to the Telemática journal's [about page](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
