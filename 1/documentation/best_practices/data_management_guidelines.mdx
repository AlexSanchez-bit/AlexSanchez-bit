# Data Management Guidelines

## Introduction

This guide provides effective data organization strategies, focusing on best practices for managing data in various contexts. The guidelines emphasize transparency, ethical considerations, and efficient data management techniques. This document aims to enhance the understanding and implementation of data management practices across different domains.

## Ethical Guidelines and Transparency

### Authorship and Contribution

- **Author Identification**: Authors must register their names with unique ORCID identifiers and institutional affiliations, including geographic location details.
- **Contribution Specification**: Manuscripts should accurately report the specific contributions of each author using the CRediT taxonomy, which includes roles such as project management, data curation, and writing.

### Conflict of Interest and Ethical Compliance

- **Conflict Disclosure**: Authors must disclose any potential conflicts of interest in their research and publication processes.
- **Ethical Standards**: Adherence to ethical guidelines is crucial, including avoiding plagiarism and ensuring the integrity of data.

## Data Center Energy Management

### Energy Efficiency Metrics

- **Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)**: While PUE is a common metric, it has limitations. Alternative metrics should be considered for a comprehensive assessment of energy efficiency.
- **Layered Architecture**: Implementing a layered architecture can support accurate, real-time energy metric collection and analysis, addressing data heterogeneity.

## Network Management Strategies

### Information-Centric Networks (ICN)

- **Efficiency and Security**: ICN architectures offer advantages over traditional IP networks, including improved efficiency, security, and scalability.
- **Integration Opportunities**: Transitioning to content-centric networking involves addressing challenges and leveraging technologies like Software-Defined Networking (SDN).

## Open Access and Data Sharing

### Open Science Practices

- **Preprints and Data Sharing**: Authors are encouraged to use preprint servers and share underlying data to promote transparency and collaboration.
- **Creative Commons Licensing**: Articles are published under Creative Commons licenses to facilitate free access and adaptation, ensuring proper credit to authors.

## Summary

This guide outlines essential data management guidelines, emphasizing ethical practices, energy efficiency in data centers, and the benefits of information-centric networking. By adhering to these strategies, organizations can improve their data management processes, enhance transparency, and contribute to the broader scientific community.

For more detailed information on these topics, please refer to the Telemática journal and its associated resources.
