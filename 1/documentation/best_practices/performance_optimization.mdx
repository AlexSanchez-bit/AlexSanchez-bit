# Performance Optimization Guide

## Introduction

This guide focuses on optimizing system performance, specifically in the context of data centers and smart antenna systems. It explores methods for energy efficiency evaluation and signal estimation improvements, leveraging innovative technologies and architectures.

## Energy Efficiency in Data Centers

### Overview

Rising energy consumption in data centers necessitates precise evaluation of energy efficiency. Traditional metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) have limitations, prompting the need for more comprehensive approaches.

### Limitations of PUE

PUE is a widely used metric for assessing data center energy performance. However, it has been critiqued for its inability to fully capture the relationship between energy consumption and service quality.

### Proposed Solutions

An integrated architecture is proposed for the collection and processing of energy metrics. This layered approach addresses the heterogeneity of energy data and supports accurate, near real-time measurements, enhancing the analysis of data center efficiency.

### Tools for Evaluation

Both commercial and open-source tools are available for estimating PUE. These tools vary in accuracy, usability, and cost-effectiveness, and should be selected based on specific data center requirements.

For more detailed information, refer to the full article: [Integrated Architecture Proposal for Data Centers](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/1025).

## Signal Estimation in Smart Antenna Systems

### Challenges

Estimating the number of incident signals in smart antenna systems is crucial for adaptively readjusting radiation patterns. Traditional methods often falter under extreme conditions such as low signal-to-noise ratios or short signal observations.

### Neural Network-Based Approach

A neural network-based preprocessing scheme is proposed to improve the estimation of incident signals. This method enhances prediction accuracy and reduces computational costs by adjusting input data ranges in real-time.

### Benefits

The proposed method allows for the application of simpler algorithms focused on signal detection, thereby improving estimation performance while reducing the need for extensive training samples.

For more detailed information, refer to the full article: [Signals Number Estimation in Smart Antennas](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/article/view/964).

## Conclusion

Optimizing system performance in data centers and smart antenna systems involves adopting innovative approaches to energy efficiency and signal estimation. By leveraging integrated architectures and neural network-based methods, organizations can achieve more accurate and cost-effective performance improvements.
