# First Steps

Welcome to the First Steps guide for navigating and interacting with the Red Sobrevivientes web application. This guide will provide you with essential actions to perform after the initial setup, ensuring you can effectively explore and utilize the platform's features.

## Introduction

The Red Sobrevivientes website offers various interactive elements and pages designed to engage users in meaningful ways. This guide outlines the primary steps to take after installation, focusing on navigating the site, interacting with key features, and understanding the available options.

## Navigating the Website

### Homepage Interaction

1. **Loading the SPA**: Allow the Single Page Application (SPA) to load by waiting for approximately 3 seconds. This ensures all interactive elements are fully available.

2. **Exploring Navigation Links**:
   - Click on the 'Sobre nosotros' link to access more information about the organization.
   - Use the 'Nuestros Aliados' link to learn about the allies associated with the platform.

### Donation Page

- **Features**: The donation page provides options for various donation amounts and integrates payment methods such as PayPal, debit, and credit cards.
- **Interactive Elements**: Users can select donation amounts and choose their preferred payment method through interactive buttons.

### Testimonial Sharing

- **Accessing the Modal**: Click the 'Sí, soy Sobreviviente.' button on the homepage to open the 'Comparte tu testimonio' modal.
- **Options**: The modal offers options to share a testimony as a survivor or as someone who knows a survivor.
- **Actions**: Users can fill out form fields and use action buttons like 'Cancelar' and 'Enviar testimonio' to manage their submissions.

## Exploring Additional Pages

### About Us

- **Access**: Navigate to the 'Sobre nosotros' page to gain insights into the organization's mission and history.
- **Documentation**: Extract and document the page's structured information for future reference.

### Blog and Statistics

- **Blog Page**: The blog section currently displays a message indicating no content is available.
- **Statistics and Map**: Explore the 'Estadísticas y mapa' page to view interactive maps and statistical data.

## Summary

This guide provides an overview of the initial steps to take after setting up the Red Sobrevivientes web application. By following these steps, users can effectively navigate the site, engage with its interactive elements, and explore the various pages and features available. For more detailed exploration, users are encouraged to interact with each section and document their findings for comprehensive understanding.

For further information and updates, visit the [Red Sobrevivientes website](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app).
