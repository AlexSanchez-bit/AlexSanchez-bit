# Contact Support

## Introduction

This guide provides information on how to contact technical support for the Red de Sobrevivientes Colombia website. The platform offers resources for survivors, including testimony submission forms, donation options, and an interactive map of crimes. It also provides contact information for further assistance.

## How to Contact Support

For any technical support or assistance related to the Red de Sobrevivientes Colombia website, you can reach out through the following contact details:

- **Email**: redsobrevivientes@protonmail.com
- **Phone**: +57 302 583 4033
- **WhatsApp**: +57 302 5834033
- **Social Media**: @redsobrevivientescol

These contact options are available for inquiries about the website's features, support resources, and any technical issues you might encounter.

## Additional Resources

The website includes several interactive elements and sections that may provide additional support:

- **Main Navigation Menu**: Explore sections like "Blog," "Estadísticas y mapa," and "Haz una donación" for more information and resources.
- **Testimony Submission Form**: Share your story or connect with others through the "Comparte tu historia" section.
- **Donation Options**: Support the organization through various donation methods, including PayPal, debit, or credit card.

## Summary

The Red de Sobrevivientes Colombia website is a comprehensive platform offering support and resources for survivors. For technical support, users can contact the organization via email, phone, or social media.
