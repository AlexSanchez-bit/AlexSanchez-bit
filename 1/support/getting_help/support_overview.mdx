# Support Overview

## Introduction

This document provides an overview of the available support options and channels for the Red Sobrevivientes platform, which offers resources and a community for survivors in Colombia. The platform is designed to facilitate support, share testimonies, and connect survivors with allies.

## Available Support Resources

### Information and Mission

The Red Sobrevivientes platform provides comprehensive information about its mission and vision, emphasizing its commitment to Colombia. Key features include:

- **Vision Statement**: A clear articulation of the organization's goals and aspirations.
- **Commitment to Colombia**: A focus on supporting Colombian survivors.
- **Six Work Axes**: Including direct legal assistance and awareness-raising activities.

For more details, visit the [Sobre nosotros](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app/#/us) page.

### Contact Information

The platform offers multiple contact methods for support and inquiries:

- **Email**: redsobrevivientes@protonmail.com
- **Phone**: +57 302 583 4033
- **Social Media**: @redsobrevivientescol on Instagram and X

### Testimony Submission

Survivors can share their stories through a dedicated testimony submission form. This form includes fields for personal details and the option to describe their experiences. For more information, visit the [Comparte tu historia](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app/#/) section.

### Allies and Community Support

The platform lists its allies, providing a network of support for survivors. Key features include:

- **Ally Listing**: Includes organizations like CRIN (home.crin.org).
- **Donation Options**: Various methods such as Donate, Pay Later, Debit, or Credit Card.

For more information, visit the [Nuestros Aliados](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app/#/) section.

## Interactive Features

The website includes several interactive elements to enhance user experience:

- **Main Navigation Menu**: Easy access to different sections of the site.
- **Buttons**: Options for survivors and those seeking information.
- **Donation Links/Buttons**: Facilitates contributions to support the cause.
- **Chat Feature**: Allows for real-time communication with the support team.

## Summary

The Red Sobrevivientes platform is a vital resource for survivors in Colombia, offering a range of support options, including legal assistance, community connections, and a platform for sharing personal stories. With robust contact options and an interactive website, it serves as a comprehensive support network for those in need.

For more information, visit the [Red Sobrevivientes website](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app).
