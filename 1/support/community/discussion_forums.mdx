# Discussion Forums

## Introduction

This documentation page provides an overview of community discussion and Q&A resources available through various platforms. It focuses on community engagement, support resources, and opportunities for users to share experiences and seek assistance.

## Community Resources

### Red Sobrevivientes Web

The Red Sobrevivientes website offers a platform for survivors in Colombia to share their testimonies, connect with allies, and access support resources. Key sections of the site include:

- **Nuestros Aliados**: A section dedicated to listing allies and providing support information. Users can explore this section to learn more about the organizations and individuals involved in supporting survivors.
- **Comparte tu historia**: This section allows users to submit their testimonies through a form. It includes fields for personal details and story submission, providing a space for survivors to share their experiences.
- **Blog**: The blog section features articles and updates related to the community, along with options for donations and a chat feature for real-time communication.

For more information, visit the [Red Sobrevivientes website](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app).

### Telemática Journal

Telemática is an open-access scientific-technical journal that publishes research and reviews in the field of Telecommunications and ICTs. It aims to advance theoretical and practical knowledge and foster technological and scientific development. The journal encourages national and international scientific discussion and is a valuable resource for academics, researchers, and students.

For more details, visit the [Telemática Journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).

## Interactive Elements

The Red Sobrevivientes website includes several interactive elements to enhance user engagement:

- **Testimony Submission Form**: Allows users to submit their stories with fields for personal details and narrative.
- **Navigation Links**: Users can easily navigate between different sections such as 'Blog', 'Estadísticas y mapa', and 'Haz una donación'.
- **Chat Feature**: Provides real-time communication options for users seeking immediate assistance or information.

## Summary

This documentation page highlights the community resources available through the Red Sobrevivientes website and the Telemática Journal. These platforms offer valuable support, information, and opportunities for engagement, catering to the needs of survivors and the academic community in the field of Telecommunications and ICTs.

For further exploration, users are encouraged to visit the respective websites and engage with the available resources and interactive features.
