# Contributing to the Project

## Introduction

This guide provides information on how to contribute to the project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities of contributors, ethical guidelines, and the process for documenting and updating project content. This document is intended to help contributors understand their roles and ensure the integrity and quality of contributions.

## Authorship and Contribution Roles

The project follows the CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) system, which defines 14 roles of authorship or contribution. These roles include:

- Project management
- Formal analysis
- Conceptualization
- Data curation
- Writing - first draft
- Writing - review and editing
- Research
- Methodology
- Obtaining funding
- Resources
- Software
- Monitoring
- Validation
- Visualization

All contributors are equally responsible for the integrity and quality of the contributions.

## Ethical Guidelines

Contributors must adhere to ethical guidelines to ensure transparency and integrity in their contributions. Key points include:

- Disclosing any potential conflicts of interest.
- Ensuring that all contributions are original and properly attributed.
- Following the IEEE standard for bibliographic references.
- Completing the Open Science Compliance Form for transparency.

## Documentation Process

The documentation process involves systematic exploration and documentation of various sections of the project. Contributors should follow these steps:

1. **Exploration and Documentation**: Navigate through different sections of the project, such as 'Nuestros Aliados', 'Comparte tu historia', and 'Blog'. Document each section in the `exploration_inventory.json` file and update the `todo.md` file to track progress.

2. **Interactive Features Testing**: Test interactive features on pages like 'Estadísticas y mapa' and 'Comparte tu historia'. Document the results and update the `results.json` file accordingly.

3. **Task Completion**: Mark tasks as complete in the `todo.md` file once documentation and testing are finished.

## Review and Feedback

Reviewers play a crucial role in maintaining the quality of contributions. They should:

- Objectively evaluate the quality of contributions.
- Respect the intellectual independence of contributors.
- Disclose any conflicts of interest.
- Notify management of any issues such as plagiarism or data falsification.

## Summary

This guide outlines the roles and responsibilities of contributors, ethical guidelines, and the process for documenting and updating project content. By following these guidelines, contributors can ensure the integrity and quality of their contributions, while reviewers help maintain these standards through objective evaluation and feedback.

For more detailed information on the ethical guidelines and submission requirements, visit [Telemática Journal](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
