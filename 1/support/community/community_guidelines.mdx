# Community Guidelines

## Introduction

This document outlines the rules and guidelines for community participation within the context of the Telemática journal. These guidelines are designed to ensure ethical practices, transparency, and integrity in scientific communication and community interactions.

## Ethical Guidelines for Authors

Authors submitting manuscripts to the Telemática journal must adhere to the following ethical guidelines:

1. **Content Relevance**: Manuscripts should present current and relevant content related to Telecommunications/ICTs, providing a concise and accurate account of the work and its scientific significance.

2. **Author Identification**: Authors must register their names with unique ORCID identifiers and include their institutional affiliations and email addresses.

3. **Manuscript Structure**: Manuscripts should follow the journal's template for structure and submission, ensuring all elements and supplementary materials are included.

4. **Author Contributions**: Specific contributions of each author must be reported using the CRediT taxonomy, covering roles such as project management, data curation, and writing.

5. **Conflict of Interest**: Authors must disclose any potential conflicts of interest related to their research.

6. **Referencing**: Bibliographic references must adhere to the IEEE standard.

7. **Preprint Servers**: Manuscripts previously deposited in preprint servers accepted by the journal are allowed, with details provided during submission.

8. **Open Science Compliance**: Authors must complete and submit the Open Science Compliance Form as a supplementary file.

## Guidelines for Reviewers

Reviewers play a crucial role in maintaining the quality and integrity of the journal. They must:

1. **Objective Evaluation**: Judge manuscripts based on their merits, promoting effective scientific communication while respecting the intellectual independence of authors.

2. **Clear Communication**: Provide clear, accurate, and objective feedback, especially when offering negative judgments.

3. **Plagiarism Detection**: Report any evidence of plagiarism or data falsification to the journal's management.

4. **Confidentiality**: Maintain the confidentiality of the manuscript content and refrain from using it without consent.

5. **Conflict of Interest**: Disclose any conflicts of interest that may affect their objectivity in the review process.

## Editorial and Ethical Standards

The Telemática journal upholds rigorous editorial and ethical standards, including:

- **Plagiarism Detection**: All submissions are checked for originality before peer review and publication.

- **Code of Ethics**: The journal adheres to the Scielo Guide of good practices and the principles of transparency recommended by COPE.

- **Open Access Policy**: The journal provides free access to its content under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 Unported License.

## Community Engagement

The Telemática journal encourages community feedback and participation, particularly in initiatives such as the selection of new logo designs for its online interface.

## Summary

The Telemática journal is committed to fostering a transparent, ethical, and collaborative community environment. By adhering to these guidelines, authors, reviewers, and community members contribute to the advancement of scientific knowledge and integrity.

For more information, visit the [Telemática journal website](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).
