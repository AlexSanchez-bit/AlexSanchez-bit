# Community Overview

Welcome to the Community Overview page. This page serves as a gateway to our user community, providing access to various resources and forums where you can connect, share, and support each other.

## Community Resources

Our community platform offers a range of resources designed to support and engage users. Here are some key sections you can explore:

### Navigation Structure

The community platform is organized into several key sections, each offering unique content and interactive elements:

- **Home**: Introduction to the organization and a call to action for survivors and interested parties.
- **Sobre nosotros**: Information about our mission and vision, including contact details.
- **Nuestros Aliados**: A list of allies and support information.
- **Comparte tu historia**: A platform for submitting personal testimonies.
- **Blog**: Latest blog entries and updates.
- **Estadísticas y mapa**: Access to statistics and maps.
- **Haz una donación**: Options for supporting the community through donations.

### Key Features

- **Interactive Elements**: The platform includes a main navigation menu, buttons for survivor status confirmation, and donation links.
- **Support Information**: Users can find contact details for support and allies, such as email addresses and social media handles.

## Community Engagement

Engage with the community by exploring the following sections:

- **Share Your Story**: Use the "Comparte tu historia" section to submit your testimony and connect with others.
- **Latest Updates**: Stay informed with the latest blog entries and organizational news.
- **Support and Allies**: Discover our network of allies and learn how you can contribute to the community.

## Summary

The community platform provides a comprehensive set of resources and interactive elements designed to support survivors and engage users. With sections dedicated to sharing stories, accessing support, and staying informed, the platform fosters a collaborative and supportive environment for all members.

For more information, visit the [Red de Sobrevivientes web application](https://red-sobrevivientes-web.netlify.app).
