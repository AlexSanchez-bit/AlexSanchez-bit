# Training Materials

## Introduction

This documentation page provides an overview of training courses and materials related to the field of Telecommunications and Information and Communication Technologies (ICT). The information is derived from the resources and publications of the Telemática journal, which is dedicated to advancing theoretical and practical knowledge in these areas.

## About Telemática Journal

The Telemática journal is an open-access scientific-technical publication that continuously updates research and reviews in Telecommunications and ICTs. It aims to foster technological and scientific development both nationally and internationally. The journal is supported by the Havana University of Technology "José Antonio Echeverría" (CUJAE).

### Focus and Scope

The journal covers a wide range of topics within Telematics and Telecommunications, including:

- Communications and wireless systems
- Emerging communication systems
- Network management and services
- Internet technologies and services
- Security in telecommunications
- Digital signal processing
- Teaching of telecommunications and telematics

For more detailed information about the journal, visit the [Telemática Journal About Page](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about).

## Publication and Access

Telemática is committed to open science practices, allowing for free access to its content. The journal supports the use of preprints, data sharing, and offers an optional transparent peer review process. All manuscripts undergo a thorough editorial, academic, and final evaluation process.

### Open Access Policy

- **Creative Commons Licensing**: Articles are published under CC-BY International, allowing for exchange, use, and adaptation with proper credit to authors.
- **Free Submission and Publication**: There are no charges for manuscript submission, processing, or publication.

For more details on the open access policy, refer to the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 Unported License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

## Author Guidelines

Authors interested in submitting to the Telemática journal must adhere to specific guidelines:

- **ORCID Requirement**: Authors must have an updated ORCID profile.
- **Manuscript Structure**: Follow the journal's template for structuring and submitting manuscripts.
- **Author Contributions**: Use the CRediT taxonomy to detail contributions.

For further submission details, visit the [Telemática Journal Submission Page](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/about/submissions).

## Community Engagement

The Telemática journal values community feedback and involvement. It recently sought input on new logo designs for its updated online interface. Community members can share their opinions via Research Gate or email.

## Summary

The Telemática journal is a key resource for those involved in Telecommunications and ICTs, providing open access to cutting-edge research and fostering community engagement. It supports open science practices and requires authors to maintain transparency and proper documentation of their contributions.

For more information on the journal's initiatives and updates, visit the [Telemática Announcements Page](https://revistatelematica.cujae.edu.cu/index.php/tele/announcement).
